What it explores

9,000+ NBA games, 2015-16 to 2022-23 (regular season and playoffs), play by play. Three themes: who performs in clutch time, how comebacks and blown leads happen, and how shot choices change late in close games.

Who it's for

NBA fans, fantasy players and analysts who want answers backed by numbers and sample sizes, not hot takes. No SQL needed.

How to use it

  • Pick an example or type your own question.
  • Answers take 30-90 seconds. The agent runs several queries.
  • Open the query cards to check its SQL and raw results.
  • Follow-ups work. It remembers the conversation.
